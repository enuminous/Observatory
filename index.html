05 / THE UNITY APPENDIX

What would count as one?

A unified framework must recover established sectors through explicit limiting maps, agree on observables in tested regimes, and confront new predictions with serious conventional alternatives.

U1 → U3
U1 / DYNAMICAL CORRESPONDENCE

Make evolution commute.

dᵢ(rᵢ(Tᵁᵗu), Tᵢᵗ(rᵢu)) ≤ εᵢ(t,u)

Define the partial reduction map, its domain, time notion, metric, and tolerance. A similar-looking equation is insufficient.

U2 / OBSERVABLE CORRESPONDENCE

Recover what instruments see.

dᵧ(Aᵢᵁ(u), Aᵢ(rᵢu)) ≤ δᵢ(u)

Specify operational observables, instrument response and uncertainty bounds before comparison.

U3 / EMPIRICAL DISCRIMINATION

Risk a measurable difference.

Δy = ycandidate − yconventional

Separate proposed novelty from ordinary alternatives, nuisance variables, and the null. Zero distinguishability is a meaningful result.
